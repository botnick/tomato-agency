## Platform

web

## Users

- **Applicants ("น้อง ๆ")** — Thai adults 18+, any gender, who want to start (or move to a new agency for)
  live streaming as a Live VJ from home. Most arrive on a phone from social media, LINE or Google search
  *(inferred from the LINE/IG funnel and keyword pages)*.
- **Searchers** — people googling งานออนไลน์ / สังกัดวีเจ / รับสมัครวีเจ who land on keyword pages and the blog.

## Product Purpose

Tomato Agency is a Thai Live VJ agency (ค่าย Live VJ). The site recruits new VJs and earns trust so the
visitor messages the team on LINE (`@tomatoagency`) to apply. Success = an applicant opens LINE and applies.

## Positioning

A small, warm agency where "พี่ ๆ ทีมงาน" personally look after each VJ: free to join, no experience needed,
work from home on your own schedule, a friendly community. Live VJ is the core service — never reposition it.

## Operating Context

Apply flow happens outside the site: LINE Official (via `go.tomato.in.th/tomato-line`), IG as a secondary
look-before-you-apply channel. The blog (weekly, trend-grounded) brings search traffic and cross-links to
keyword pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Eleventy static site on Cloudflare Pages; `git push` to main auto-deploys (owner reviews before push).
- Every outbound link goes through a `go.tomato.in.th` shortlink; JSON-LD keeps real profile URLs.
- No platform is featured on brand pages. IDOL+ was removed entirely on 2026-10-21 (owner order).
  Bigo/TikTok may appear in blog posts and SEO keyword pages only.
- No splash / preloader screen (owner, 2026-10-21: "ไม่เอา preload ละ รก").

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Tomato Agency" (capital T in prose). Logo `assets/img/logo.png` (round red tomato, to/ma/to),
  wordmark `assets/img/wordmark.png`. The logo keeps tomato red; the UI accent is rose pink.
- Look: the original light, cute, feminine site, refined (owner, 2026-10-21: "คง ui คล้าย ๆ เดิม มีความเป็นผู้หญิงหน่อย",
  "มีความน่ารัก", reference vibie.live). A dark themed concept redesign was rejected as "ai slop".
- Voice: friendly, a bit cute; "พี่ ๆ ทีมงาน" for the team, "น้อง ๆ" for applicants; soft particles นะ / จ้า are fine.
- LINE `@tomatoagency`, IG `@tomatoagency.th`, domain `tomato.in.th`, founded 2024.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: LINE/IG handles, founding year, privacy page, blog posts with original covers. Existing testimonial
cards (Mintra, Praewa, Aira) are the owner's copy. **Absent and never to be invented:** income figures,
follower counts, VJ counts, guarantees, new testimonials or statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Get the visitor to LINE with as little friction as possible.
2. Honest and warm over hype — no money promises, no fabricated proof.
3. Live VJ first; the site never sells another company's app.
4. Thai readability first: Thai text never set in italic display serif.
